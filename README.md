# Business Mileage Tracker 1.0.8

This update streamlines the Reports screen for mobile:

- Keeps the year-to-date summary at the top.
- Places year, business entity, and drive type filters in one compact row.
- Collapses monthly details by default to reduce scrolling.
- Lets each month expand only when its details are needed.
- Applies entity and drive-type filters consistently to monthly results.

Install over the existing app. Do not uninstall the app or clear its data before updating.
